# Value Stream Mapping

## See how work flows. Choose what to improve.

I created this guided review for consultants working with clients and teams reviewing their own service. Start with who you serve and the result they need. Choose one workflow, map what happens today, then decide what is worth testing.

## When to use it

- Before redesigning a service, writing an SOP or choosing automation.
- When work gets stuck between people or tools and the cause is unclear.
- When several offers compete for attention and the team needs to choose where to look first.

Rough notes are enough to begin. The people doing the work must check the map. No other skill or mapping software is required.

## What you get

An offers overview or a provisional workflow map, evidence gaps and the next decision. Once the team chooses an improvement, keep the current state, next pilot and longer-term ideal separate.

The skill does not prove a bottleneck from notes or promise savings. If the team has not selected a workflow, it stops at the overview. Client examples stay separate from the public skill.
