# Interview, Draft, Edit

## Use AI to organize your thoughts. Keep your voice.

I created this skill to help you work out what you want to say. You bring an idea in writing or speech. AI asks questions, organizes your answers into a draft, and marks its suggestions. You make the final edit.

## When to use it

- You have a point to make, but the draft skips the example or reasoning behind it.
- You can explain an idea aloud more easily than you can write it.
- You want help with structure without letting AI supply your opinions or experiences.

Bring a rough idea, draft or transcript. Writing samples are optional. No knowledge graph or other skill is required.

## What you get

A focused interview, a proposed outline, then a draft when you are ready. Suggested connecting language and conclusions appear in brackets for your review. You check facts, accept or cut additions and make the final edit.

The skill does not guarantee that a draft sounds like you. It cannot know a belief or experience you have not shared. It stops before publication.
