## An update after a call

This example is invented to show the shape of the exchange. The company, the person and the details are not real, and it is not a record of a customer conversation.

### What the user says

> Just got off with Dana at Harbour Print. They like the idea but the owner is away until the 3rd. She said "send me something short I can forward to him". They are on the old plan still. I should follow up after he is back.

### What the assistant proposes

**Record found:** Harbour Print Co. (one match on name and domain). Dana Reyes is linked as a contact.

| Change | Proposed |
| --- | --- |
| New note | Title: Dana wants a short summary to forward to the owner |
| Stage | Contacted → In conversation |
| New task | Send Dana a short summary. Due the 4th. Owner: you |

**The note, as it would be saved**

**What they said**

- The owner is away until the 3rd.

**What we saw**

**Plan**

**Not changed:** the plan field. Your notes confirm the current plan and do not say it changed.

**I have not written anything yet. Approve all three, or tell me what to change.**

### What happens next

The user answers "yes, but make the task due the 5th". The assistant writes the note, the stage and the task with the new date, then reads each one back from Attio and shows what is saved.

### What to notice

- The assistant matched one record and said how. With two matches it would have asked.
- The quote stayed a quote.
- Nothing appeared under What we saw that the user or Attio did not supply.
- The assistant drafted no email and sent nothing.
