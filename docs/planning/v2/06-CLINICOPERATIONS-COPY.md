### The things we want to understand

- Keeping work moving. How do follow-ups, billing questions and handoffs get a clear owner?
- Supporting the team. What helps people do their work well without another layer of administration?
- Knowing what helped. Which numbers and conversations lead to a useful decision?

Small line: Owners, managers, front-desk, billing and operations teams are welcome. Whether you use Jane or another system, your experience matters.

### Five questions for your next clinic check-in

A starting point for a team conversation:

1. What went better this week, and what helped?
2. Where did work get stuck?
3. What needs a clear owner?
4. What one change will we try next?
5. How will we know whether it helped?

## About & Participate

Title: About and participate · Clinic Operations
H1: Let's learn from the work itself.

Clinic Operations is for people who want to run their clinic well and learn from others doing the same. That includes a single clinic, a small team, and groups working across locations.

### Who is behind this

I'm Rob Saric, a founder in Ottawa. I build Caretrics and spend time with clinic teams to understand the operational work that gets in their way.

Caretrics is my software business. Clinic Operations is where I am starting conversations and sharing lessons from that work. That relationship will be visible wherever it is relevant. You do not need to use Caretrics to take part.

Links: More about Rob → https://robsaric.com/about/ ; Caretrics → https://caretrics.com/

### Take part (`id="take-part"`)

Bring one thing that is working well or one problem you are trying to solve. We can spend about 20 minutes comparing notes, or you can send a few thoughts by email.

I'll send you a short recap afterward. If there is something worth sharing more widely, I'll check the wording and attribution with you first.

Button: Email Rob to compare notes

Destination: `mailto:rob@caretrics.com?subject=Clinic%20Operations%3A%20compare%20notes`. Display `rob@caretrics.com` next to the button so visitors can copy it if they do not use a mail app.

Helper: Tell me your role, the kind of clinic you work with, and what you would like to talk about. Please leave out patient names, records and screenshots.

### What to expect

- A conversation about your work. There is no product demo on the agenda.
- No recording by default. If recording would help, I will ask first.
- Your name, clinic and quotes will only be published with your approval. We can discuss an unnamed example, but we will still check that the details do not identify your clinic or anyone in it.
- Taking part does not add you to a newsletter or sales sequence. If you would like future notes, tell me and we can arrange that separately.

## Privacy

Title/H1: Privacy

Clinic Operations is run by Rob Saric, founder of Caretrics. This page describes the initial version of the site and its email-based conversations.

### Information you share

If you email me, I receive your email address and the information you include. I use that information to reply, arrange a conversation, and prepare the recap or other material we agree on. Please do not send patient information.

### Conversations and publication

I may keep working notes to prepare a recap. I do not record conversations by default. Before publishing your name, clinic or quotes, I will ask you to approve the wording and attribution. Taking part does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

### Website hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Hosting services may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. This initial site has no advertising trackers, embedded scheduling tools or newsletter signup form.

### Questions or removal requests

Email rob@caretrics.com if you have questions about information you have shared or would like me to remove conversation notes I hold. We can also discuss corrections or removal of material published here.
