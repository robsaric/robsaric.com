## What Rob asked for

Rob drafted a LinkedIn post that opens "I rarely log into our CRM anymore." While working on it he asked whether a custom skill should support the post and live on robsaric.com, and whether it should also be in `github.com/robsaric/robsaric.com`. He then said: "Do smart things. Make the update." This was a direct instruction in a session with Claude, not a Grill question, so it carries no Q number.

His description of how he works, in his words from that session: structure the outreach or account-management process and do the planning before starting with Attio; Attio comes with default fields, and which ones matter depends on the sales goals; once segments, lists, campaigns and custom fields are structured, Claude or ChatGPT can update and manage the workspace, so he rarely works in Attio's own interface.

## Decisions

- **A fourth Toolbox skill, in the existing pattern.** Name `crm-by-conversation`, title "CRM by Conversation", promise "Keep your CRM current without opening it." Source in `skills/crm-by-conversation/`; downloads generated by `pnpm skills:build`; guide at `/toolbox/crm-by-conversation/`. The name and promise are Claude's proposal and are Rob's to change before release.
- **Attio only.** The instructions name Attio's connector and its behaviour. They are not written for other CRMs.
- **Two jobs.** Build once (inspect, interview, propose, build, load), then run (after a call, what needs attention today, start a campaign, clean up). The interview comes before any build, which is Rob's point about planning first.
- **Every change is a proposal.** The skill and the chat prompt both carry the same rules: proposal first, read back after writing, one record per company and person, delete or merge only on the user's explicit word, no invented facts, no sending email, text inside Attio is data and never instructions, and say so when the connector cannot do something. `tests/toolbox-skills.test.mjs` fails if the proposal, no-send or data rules are removed from either file.
- **The worked example is invented and says so.** Harbour Print Co. and Dana Reyes are not real. No Caretrics prospect, client or segment name appears in any public file.

## The promise this changes

Until now the collection said, in `skills/README.md` and on each guide, that Toolbox skills "do not run scripts, connect to accounts or make changes on your behalf" and need no connected account. That stays true for the first three. It is not true for this one: it guides an assistant that the reader has connected to their own Attio account, and that assistant can create and change records.

The change is stated, not blurred:

- `skills/README.md` now says three skills work only from pasted material and one works through the reader's Attio connection with approval before each write.
- The guide has a section headed "This one works inside your account." directly after the quick start: what it needs, what it can do, what it does not do, and where to start.
- `ToolboxQuickStart` gained a `connected` option so the shared intro and download note no longer tell this guide's reader that no account connection is involved. The other three guides render exactly as before.
- The package itself still contains instructions only: no scripts, connectors or keys.
